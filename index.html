I am a PhD scholar at IIT Hyderabad advised by Dr. Maria Francis. My research focuses on the design and analysis of privacy-preserving authentication schemes in the classical setting. I am also interested in post-quantum privacy-preserving alternatives based on lattice-based primitives.
