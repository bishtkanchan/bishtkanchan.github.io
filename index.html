Kanchan Bisht

Ph.D. Student, Computer Science & Engineering

Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad

I am a Ph.D. researcher in cryptography at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad, advised by Dr. Maria Francis. My research focuses on the design and analysis of privacy-preserving authentication and revocation mechanisms that balance strong privacy guarantees with accountability. My work spans blind signatures, anonymous credentials, and revocation mechanisms in the classical cryptographic setting, while I am also interested in post-quantum cryptography, particularly lattice-based approaches for privacy-preserving authentication.
